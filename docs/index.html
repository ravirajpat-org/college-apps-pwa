College Apps

Texas schools (in-state tuition), ranked by program reputation + overall selectivity, now evaluated for both Computer Science AND Aerospace Engineering fit. Texas-specific factor that changes strategy: the state's automatic-admission rule is based on class rank percentile, not raw GPA — top 6% guarantees admission to UT Austin, top 10% to Texas A&M (for TX residents meeting course requirements). But auto-admission to the university does NOT guarantee placement in either the CS or Aerospace major at these two schools — both are separately competitive on top of that. Class rank, not just GPA, is worth tracking closely from here on.

Aerospace changes the picture meaningfully: Rice, UT Dallas, and SMU have no dedicated undergraduate Aerospace Engineering major (Rice offers an aerospace track within Mechanical Engineering only) — Texas A&M and UT Austin are the two real in-state options for aerospace specifically. Added UT Arlington below since it's the one additional Texas school with a genuine dedicated Aerospace Engineering degree.

Columbia Engineering Different kind of path

Combined Plan (3-2) · Fu Foundation School of Engineering and Applied Science, New York · BA + BS, 5 years total

This one is not a high-school application target. She'd apply as a freshman to one of ~100 affiliate liberal-arts partner colleges (e.g. Middlebury, Goucher, Fordham), spend 3 years there, then apply into Columbia Engineering during the winter of her junior year of college. The actual Columbia admission event happens two years after high school, not during it.

Structure3 years at the partner college (BA coursework) + 2 years at Columbia Engineering (BS) = two degrees, 5 years total
Prerequisite courseworkFull Calculus sequence (through multivariable/Calc III–IV), 2–3 semesters Physics, General Chemistry + one lab, one Computer Science course, Intro Economics, ~27 nontechnical credits — completed at the partner college
GPA to transfer~3.0+ minimum overall and in pre-engineering coursework (exact bar varies by partner school — confirm with the specific affiliate)
ApplicationSubmitted via Columbia's application in winter of junior year of college · opens December, preferred deadline mid-Feb, final mid-March, decisions mid-April, fall entry only
Aerospace fitNot yet available as a major. Columbia Engineering currently only offers an Aerospace Engineering minor (first graduates were this year); a full major, master's concentration, and grad program are "expected in coming semesters" but not live yet. Don't plan around Columbia for Aerospace until the major actually launches.

Prep plan: the real near-term decision isn't about Columbia at all — it's picking a strong affiliate partner college as her actual senior-year application target, the same way she'd apply to any other school · once enrolled, front-load Calculus/Physics/Chemistry/CS in the first 3 years to hit the prerequisite list above · keep GPA comfortably above 3.0, ideally 3.3+, since pre-engineering coursework GPA is weighted separately and scrutinized · get the exact affiliate list and partner-specific terms from Columbia Engineering's Combined Plan office once a shortlist of colleges forms.

GPASuccessful applicants typically 3.7+; American-curriculum admits closer to 3.8+ unweighted

Acceptance rate~4% — one of the most selective universities globally, more selective than NYU's New York campus
